    Annotate proteins to begin

    1. Upload a multi-FASTA file of hypothetical proteins, paste sequences, or load the demo.
    2. Choose embeddings (offline hashed for speed, ESM-2 for precision) and press Annotate.
    3. Select a protein to inspect its sequence, property tracks, GO terms and homology.

    Ambiguous residues (X, B, Z, U, O) are substituted and masked automatically.
